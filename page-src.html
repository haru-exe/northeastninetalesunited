Notation rules (Sadd's)

  • Indices $i,j,k,\dots$ run over $1,2,3$; a repeated index is summed; a comma means a partial derivative, $u_{i,j}=\partial u_i/\partial x_j$. Coordinates $x_1,x_2,x_3$ are also written $x,y,z$.
  • Displacement $u_i=(u,v,w)$. Strain tensor $e_{ij}$ with components $e_x,e_y,e_z,e_{xy},e_{yz},e_{zx}$; engineering shear strain $\gamma_{xy}=2e_{xy}$. Rotation tensor $\omega_{ij}$, rotation vector $\omega_i$.
  • Stress $\sigma_{ij}$ with components $\sigma_x,\sigma_y,\sigma_z,\sigma_{xy},\sigma_{yz},\sigma_{zx}$ (Sadd writes shear stress as $\sigma_{xy}$, not $\tau_{xy}$). Traction $\mathbf{T}^{n}$ or $T_i^{n}$; body force per unit volume $F_i$.
  • Rotation of axes: $Q_{ij}=\cos(x_i',x_j)$; primes mark components in the rotated axes.
  • Invariants: $I_a, II_a, III_a$ for a general tensor $a_{ij}$; $\vartheta_1,\vartheta_2,\vartheta_3$ for strain, with dilatation $\vartheta=e_{kk}$; $I_1,I_2,I_3$ for stress. Principal stresses ranked $\sigma_1\ge\sigma_2\ge\sigma_3$.
  • Spherical parts carry a tilde, $\tilde e_{ij}$; deviatoric parts a hat, $\hat e_{ij}$.
  • Elastic constants $\lambda$ (Lamé), $\mu$ (shear modulus, some texts write $G$), $E$, $\nu$, $k$ (bulk modulus); elasticity tensor $C_{ijkl}$; thermal expansion $\alpha$, temperature change $T-T_0$. Note: $\lambda$ also names an eigenvalue in Topic 2; each topic says which it means.
  • Signs: normal stress and normal strain are positive in tension or extension; a shear stress is positive when it acts on a positive face in a positive direction; shear strain is positive when the right angle between the positive axes decreases.
  • Units: stress in $\mathrm{Pa}=\mathrm{N\,m^{-2}}$ (usually $\mathrm{MPa}$ or $\mathrm{GPa}$); strain is dimensionless, often quoted in units of $10^{-6}$.
